# What is open, with the programme finished

Waves 17 to 24 have all run and every parcel is retired. This is what is left,
in the order it costs something to leave alone.

## Needs you, not code

**The two testimonials are not published, and will not be until you confirm
them.** `TESTIMONIALS_CONFIRMED` in `shared/testimonials.ts` is `false`. The two
quotations were transcribed by an agent from the public Upwork agency profile,
and `upwork.com` answers 403 to anything that is not a browser, so no automatic
check can confirm them — I tried. Open the profile, read the two quotations word
for word, and flip the flag. Until then both home pages look exactly as they did.

**`/admin` will refuse you.** The allow-list is empty: neither `OPERATOR_EMAIL`
nor `OPERATOR_LINKEDIN_SUB` is set on the deployment, and the house fallback is
`LEAD_NOTIFY_EMAIL` = dan@being.marketing, which can only sign in if that
address already has a room. Simplest path: open `/admin`, take the 403, read the
server log in Render — the refusal prints the identities your account holds —
and paste the `linkedin:` value into `OPERATOR_LINKEDIN_SUB`.

**Nobody has tried the visitor calendar end to end.** The redirect URI is
registered and Google accepts the authorize request; what has not been done is a
person pressing the link in the booking popup and watching a busy slot come back
marked.

**`docs/case-studies.pdf` is still tracked** under the old name, superseded by
the one named the way you asked, and referenced by nothing. Deleting it is one
command; it is your file, so it is your call.

## Known and deliberate

**LinkedIn services are hidden** behind `shared/linkedin-review.ts` while the
API application is read. One constant turns them back on, and every mention on
both sites follows it.

**adgrant.ai has no LinkedIn sign-in.** LinkedIn returns to one address, the one
registered on the app, and that is top-rated.team. Giving adgrant.ai its own way
in means its own LinkedIn app and its own credentials. WhatsApp and the emailed
link both work there today.

**The promo animations are as the owner found them** — the direction is numbers
rather than images, which is a choice in `scripts/promo/copy.ts` and not a limit
of the renderer. Left alone at his instruction.

## Reviews that did not finish

The wave-21 adversarial pass lost 63 of 86 agents to the monthly spend limit.
Four findings were confirmed and fixed; an unknown share of the 23 counted
"refuted" were never actually checked, because a claim whose three verifiers all
failed to start counts as refuted in that arithmetic.

Waves 22, 23 and 24 had no adversarial pass at all, for the same reason. They
were read by hand, and what that found is in the commits: a real server-side
gate on the template files, a sound allow-list on the admin page with three
repairs around it, and a testimonials section that was built for both sites and
placed on one.

## The shape of the thing now

Two sites from one process. 680 tests. Fifteen tables in the database, all
matching what the code writes through. Three ways into a room, one account
behind them, a session that survives a redeploy. A booking that can be returned
to, changed and cancelled. adgrant.ai as the first in-house fork, with a written
answer for a fork of a fork. A people page only its operator can open.
