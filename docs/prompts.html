WHAT DONE MEANS FOR THIS PARCEL:
  A BOOKING YOU CAN COME BACK TO, CHANGE, AND CANCEL. Read docs/review/next-waves.md, section
  booking-return — it is the owner's own words and it is the specification. (1) THE BROWSER
  REMEMBERS. A successful booking is recorded in the site's own session, and pressing Book a
  call again opens the confirmation rather than the picker. THAT FLAG IS A POINTER, NOT A
  CREDENTIAL: on its own it must never be enough to read or change a booking, because a cookie
  is copyable and a booking carries somebody's name, time and address. The server decides what
  may be shown, every time. (2) TWO LINKS IN THE CONFIRMATION: change, and cancel. (3) A WAY
  BACK FROM ANYWHERE for a WhatsApp booking — a short address the owner writes as
  https://top-rated.team/<code> that opens the site with that booking's popup already open. The
  code IS the credential there, so treat it as one: it expires with the booking, it is
  single-purpose, and it is never logged. Decide deliberately whether a bare /<code> at the site
  root can collide with any existing route, and say what you decided. (4) CLEARING IT.
  Cancelling clears the flag; so does the call time passing. Neither can be relied on — the flag
  is per browser and a person may cancel from another device — so the SERVER's answer decides
  what the popup shows and the flag is only a hint about what to ask for. (5) CANCELLING DELETES
  THE EVENT. Where there is an address, let Google tell them. Where the booking was proved by
  WhatsApp, the message goes to the chat that proved it and nowhere else, and never to a chat
  that did not. (6) CHANGING IS A CANCEL AND A BOOK, in that order, and the new slot is
  re-checked as free before the old one is released — losing somebody's slot while moving it is
  worse than refusing the move.

WHY IT EXISTS, so you can judge the edge cases yourself:
  The owner booked through the QR code and it worked, and then everything after it was missing.

  to change the time and no way to cancel; and somebody who booked from a phone and closed the
  tab has no way back to the booking at all. A booking a person cannot get out of is a booking
  they will simply not attend, and the calendar keeps the slot.
