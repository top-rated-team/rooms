  is the whole credential for that hold. The message goes to the number and account in the brief
  and arrives through server/unipile/inbound.ts, which already applies the seven checks —
  register a matcher there, do not grow a second webhook. (3) CONFIRM. When the matcher fires,
  create the event, and only then show the confirmation with the Meet link. The popup already
  polls GET /api/booking/confirmed; what changes is what that endpoint means — it now reports
