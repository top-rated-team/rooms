## Why New York Animal Shelters Should Use the Google Ad Grant

If you manage an animal shelter or rescue in New York, the Google Ad Grant can put up to **$10,000 in free Google Search ads every month** behind your mission. That’s roughly $329 a day to promote adoptable pets, recruit fosters and volunteers, drive spay/neuter sign-ups, or raise emergency funds—all without spending your limited budget.

We've worked on 4,539 processed Ad Grant accounts, including many animal rescues, and nonprofits in NYC see strong results with the Grant when it’s used strategically. The local demand for pet adoption and rescue is high, and New Yorkers frequently search for things like “animal shelter near me”, “adopt a dog NYC”, or “foster a cat New York.” Capturing that intent with targeted ads can make a real difference.

### What Does $10,000/mo in Google Ads Look Like for Your Shelter?

- The Grant allows up to **$329 per day** in search ad spend.
- Default campaigns have a **$2 maximum cost-per-click (CPC)** cap, so you can expect roughly **160 clicks daily** on average.
- Average click-through rates (CTR) for well-managed Grant accounts run at **5% or higher**; falling below risks account suspension.
- With good conversion tracking, you can remove the $2 CPC cap using Smart Bidding strategies like Maximize Conversions, helping you drive more meaningful actions like adoption inquiries or volunteer sign-ups.

In a dense, competitive market like New York City, these clicks translate into thousands of local potential adopters, fosters, and donors seeing your shelter at the exact moment they’re searching.

### How to Target New Yorkers Searching for Animal Help

Google Search is by far the most popular way people in NYC find local nonprofits. When you’re targeting your ads, it’s crucial to include local keywords and phrases that reflect the way New Yorkers search:

**Example keyword themes:**
- animal shelter New York
- adopt a dog NYC
- foster a pet near me
- spay and neuter clinic New York
- animal rescue Manhattan
- humane society Brooklyn
- emergency pet medical fund NYC

**Sample ad copy angles:**
- "Looking to Adopt a Dog in NYC? Visit [Your Shelter] Today!"
- "Foster a Cat Near You – Help NYC Pets Find a Home"
- "Affordable Spay & Neuter Services in New York – Book Now"
- "Support NYC Wildlife Rescue – Donate to Emergency Medical Fund"

Using location-specific terms like “New York,” “NYC,” “Manhattan,” or borough-specific names in your ads and keywords will help you appear for those high-intent local searches.

### Best Practices to Keep Your Google Ad Grant Account Running Smoothly

Since Google requires at least a **5% CTR monthly**, your ads need to be relevant and engaging. Neglecting this can lead to suspension. Here’s what we recommend:

1. Use **2+ word keywords** that are highly relevant to animal shelter services and local intent.
2. Write **at least 2 ads per ad group** and have **2 or more ad groups per campaign**.
3. Set up **conversion tracking** for actions like adoption form fills, volunteer sign-ups, or donation completions.
4. Use **location targeting** to focus on New York City neighborhoods where your potential adopters and supporters live (/glossary/geographic-targeting-google-ad-grant).
5. Consider Smart Bidding strategies to remove the $2 CPC cap and get more conversions.
