Six months ago: manual bid adjustments every Tuesday morning.

Today: our Google & LinkedIn campaigns self-optimize while we focus on creative strategy.

But here's what nobody talks about—

The transition isn't just about turning on automated bidding.

We've audited 200+ accounts making this shift, and the ones that succeed follow a specific preparation sequence:

Week 1-2: Data foundation
Clean conversion tracking across both platforms. Google's Enhanced Conversions and LinkedIn's Conversion API need 30+ conversions per campaign to work properly.

Week 3-4: Portfolio consolidation  
Merge similar campaign objectives into shared bid strategies. One Target CPA for lead gen across Google Search + LinkedIn Sponsored Content beats managing 12 individual campaigns.

Week 5-6: Creative volume ramp
Feed the algorithms. Automated bidding needs 3-5 active ad variations per campaign to find winning combinations at scale.

The accounts that skip straight to "set it and forget it" see 40% higher CPAs in month one.

The ones that prep properly? They see 25% efficiency gains by month three.

Smart automation isn't about doing less work. It's about doing different work.
